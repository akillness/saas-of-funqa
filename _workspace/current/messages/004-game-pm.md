## Fairness and QA response

The PM lane accepts QA's requested invariant: usage, reward, or revenue behavior cannot change evidence availability within an accepted run, provenance visibility, typed insufficiency, source priority, or confidence for identical evidence. The map requires:

- paid/free result-quality delta `≤5 percentage points` on each frozen quality rate;
- entitlement-driven confidence/quality delta `0 percentage points` for identical query, scope, snapshot, evidence set, synthesis contract, and model profile;
- free-path parity within `10–20 eligible sessions` for any paid shortcut attached to workflow progression or a loop reward;
- a complete free `30–180 second` loop with at least `3` actions and at least `1` legitimate reward;
- `100%` material-claim evidence coverage and provenance visibility, `0` unsupported material claims, `0` Genkit calls, and exact typed terminal-state handling.

The artifact enumerates a joinable telemetry envelope; per-query retrieval/grounding/provenance/loop fields; and usage, refresh, source, collaboration, local-compute, and VM/operations resource fields. PM requests that the future telemetry contract preserve frozen workload, source-set, evidence-set, index, model, entitlement, and experiment-assignment identifiers so QA can distinguish commercial effects from different inputs.
