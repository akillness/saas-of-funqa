# Triage
- Problem: FunQA needs one evidence-first game-log search workspace whose composition, controls, provenance, failure states, and repeat-search loop are legible before the non-Genkit vertical slice is designed or implemented.
- Audience: Game creators, game researchers, and game operators using a browser/Next.js workspace backed by CocoIndex retrieval/provenance and local-model evidence-grounded synthesis.
- Why now: Stage 1 Phase 1a must calibrate G7 loop inputs and G8 novelty inputs before Phase 1b. The next public beat is Firebase App Hosting production deployment after push, but no gate verdict is established by this survey.

## Survey Mode

- Mode: `market-landscape`
- Run: `20260809-game-log-agentic-search`
- Bounded question: Which composition, controls, evidence, failure-state, and repeat-search patterns should govern a game-log search workspace?
- Comparable set: OP.GG, Mobalytics, Tracker Network, SteamDB, Perplexity, and Gemini Notebook (six products counted consistently).
- Evidence cutoff: 2026-08-09.

## Inclusion and Exclusion

Included products expose at least one relevant search behavior: player/game-log inspection, historical game-data browsing, cited answer synthesis, source-scoped research, or explicit insufficiency handling. Product claims are limited to current official product/help pages recovered directly unless labeled `thin evidence`.

Excluded from the count are generic chat assistants without visible provenance, game storefront search without a meaningful detail workspace, and implementation frameworks. CocoIndex and the local model are architecture owners for FunQA, not market comparables.

## Decision Boundary

This packet recommends survey-derived inputs only. It does not write Phase 1b concept, core-loop, novelty-scorecard, balance-sheet, worldview, or presentation artifacts. It does not change the accepted boundary: CocoIndex owns ingestion, index refresh, retrieval, and provenance; the local model owns planning and synthesis from returned evidence; Genkit is neither owner nor fallback.

## Evidence Status

- Direct official evidence is available for all six comparable home/product surfaces.
- Strong help-document evidence is available for SteamDB search controls, Perplexity citations and prompt refinement, and Gemini Notebook source selection, citation jumps, retained chat, limits, and no-answer causes.
- Advanced match-history filters for OP.GG, Mobalytics, and Tracker were not consistently recoverable as stable official help documentation; filter granularity beyond the directly visible product pages is not used as a high-confidence frequency claim.
- Gate status: not evaluated. No G2, G7, or G8 verdict is issued here.
